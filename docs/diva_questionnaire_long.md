# Daily Functioning Questionnaire

**Scale:**
* 1 = Not at all
* 2 = A little
* 3 = Somewhat
* 4 = Quite a lot
* 5 = Very much / all day

## Part 1 - Attention and organisation (DIVA O1-O9)
1. I made mistakes because I rushed or did not read something properly: emails, forms, instructions, work tasks. (O1)
2. I had to re-read, restart, or go back over something because I lost focus partway through. (O2)
3. I lost track of what someone was saying while they were talking directly to me. (O3)
4. I started tasks but moved on before finishing them. (O4)
5. I struggled to plan or structure my time: I was late, double-booked, or did not know where to begin. (O5)
6. I put off or avoided a task because it required sustained concentration. (O6)
7. I spent time looking for something I had misplaced: keys, phone, wallet, papers. (O7)
8. I was pulled away from what I was doing by noise, notifications, or things happening around me. (O8)
9. I forgot something I was supposed to do, bring, or respond to. (O9)

## Part 2 - Inner restlessness and impulsivity (DIVA H/I 1-9)
1. I felt physically restless: fidgeting, bouncing my leg, picking at my nails or hair. (H/I 1)
2. I found it hard to stay seated or still when I was expected to. (H/I 2)
3. I felt an inner restlessness, or found it hard to relax and do nothing. (H/I 3)
4. I found it difficult to settle into something quiet: reading, watching something, sitting still. (H/I 4)
5. My thoughts felt like they were running without stopping. (H/I 5)
6. I talked more than I intended, or found it hard to stop once I had started. (H/I 6)
7. I said something before thinking it through. (H/I 7)
8. I felt impatient when I had to wait: in a queue, in traffic, for my turn to speak. (H/I 8)
9. I interrupted someone or cut into a conversation or activity. (H/I 9)

## Part 3 - Emotional regulation and daily functioning
1. My mood shifted quickly or felt harder to control than usual.
2. I felt sensitive to criticism or rejection, or spent time wondering whether I had upset someone.
3. I worked hard to appear more organised or more focused than I actually felt.
4. Ordinary tasks took more effort than they usually do.
5. I felt overwhelmed by things that would not normally overwhelm me.
6. My medication felt effective. *(Reverse direction: 5 = worked very well)*
7. I experienced adverse side effects (e.g. trouble sleeping, heart-racing, headaches, loss of appetite, nauseau, anxiety)
8. [If the patient answered yes to have experienced side effects] I experienced the following side effects. (answer is not a scale, but a multiple choice with the following options: trouble sleeping, heart-racing, headaches, loss of appetite, nauseau, anxiety, other)

# DIVA-inspired questionnaire (short version, 14 items)

## Scale

1 = Not at all
2 = A little
3 = Somewhat
4 = Quite a lot
5 = Very much / all day

## Part 1 - Attention and organisation (DIVA O1-O9)

1. I made careless mistakes or had to go back over things because I lost focus partway through. (O1, O2)
2. My attention drifted: in conversations, or when noise, notifications or things around me pulled me away. (O3, O8)
3. I avoided, put off, or didn't finish tasks that required sustained concentration. (O4, O6)
4. I struggled to plan or structure my time: I was late, double-booked, or did not know where to begin. (O5)
5. I forgot or misplaced things: tasks, appointments, messages, keys, phone. (O7, O9)

## Part 2 - Inner restlessness and impulsivity (DIVA H/I 1-9)

6. I felt physically restless: fidgeting, or finding it hard to stay seated or still. (H/I 1, H/I 2)
7. I felt restless inside: hard to relax or settle into something quiet, or my thoughts kept racing. (H/I 3, H/I 4, H/I 5)
8. I talked more than I meant to, or said things before thinking them through. (H/I 6, H/I 7)
9. I was impatient when waiting, or interrupted others. (H/I 8, H/I 9)

## Part 3 - Emotional regulation and daily functioning

10. My mood shifted quickly, or I felt unusually sensitive to criticism or rejection.
11. Ordinary tasks, including keeping up appearances, took more effort than usual or felt overwhelming.
12. My medication felt effective. *(Reverse direction: 5 = worked very well)*
13. I experienced adverse side effects (e.g. trouble sleeping, heart-racing, headaches, loss of appetite, nauseau, anxiety)
14. [If the patient answered yes to have experienced side effects] I experienced the following side effects. (answer is not a scale, but a multiple choice with the following options: trouble sleeping, heart-racing, headaches, loss of appetite, nauseau, anxiety, other)
